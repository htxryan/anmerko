---
title: Screenshot comments
description: Capture a visible region and add feedback to it.
---

Use a screenshot comment when the feedback applies to a visual region rather than one selectable element.

1. Position the page, open **More Comment Options** beside **Select Element**, and choose **Take Screenshot**.
2. Drag over the region you want to capture. Move the box or drag its corners to adjust it.
3. Choose **Use Screenshot**, write your feedback, and save it.

With the keyboard, arrow keys move the box. Focus a corner and use the arrow keys to resize it; hold **Shift** to move or resize by ten pixels. **Cancel** or **Esc** discards the capture. Switching tabs, scrolling, or resizing the viewport during capture also cancels it.

![Screenshot feedback about a recipe card on Salad Recipe Finder](/screenshots/02-screenshot-feedback.png)

**Locate** returns to the saved region. anmerko stores only the cropped PNG, not the full-page capture. PNGs are limited to 2,400 pixels on the longest side and about 2 MB each, subject to browser storage limits.

When you [send screenshot feedback to your agent](/docs/send-to-your-agent/), download the ZIP and attach the matching PNG files separately. Review each crop before sharing because it includes everything visible in the selected region, including form values and embedded frames.
