---
title: Screenshot comments
description: Capture a visible region or the full page and add feedback to it.
---

Use a screenshot comment for feedback about a visual region.

1. Position the page and choose **Take Screenshot**.
2. Drag over the region you want to capture. Move the box or drag its corners to adjust it.
3. Choose **Use Screenshot**, write your feedback, and save it.

To capture the whole page instead, choose **Full Page** after step 1. anmerko scrolls from the top of the page to the bottom, capturing one screen at a time, then returns to where you were. Fixed and sticky elements such as headers appear only once, at the top. Keep the tab in view until the comment editor opens; this takes about two-thirds of a second per screen of page. Very long pages are scaled down to fit in one image. Pages taller than about 65,000 pixels are captured from the top until the image reaches that limit. Content inside separately scrolling panels is captured as it currently appears.

With the keyboard, arrow keys move the box. Focus a corner and use the arrow keys to resize it; hold **Shift** to move or resize by ten pixels. **Cancel** or **Esc** discards the capture. Switching tabs, scrolling, or resizing the viewport during capture also cancels it.

![Screenshot feedback about a recipe card on Salad Recipe Finder](/screenshots/02-screenshot-feedback.png)

**Locate** returns to the saved region, or to the top of the page for a full-page screenshot. anmerko stores only the cropped PNG or the stitched full-page image, not the frames used to build them. Region PNGs are limited to 2,400 pixels on the longest side and about 2 MB each; full-page images are saved as PNG, or as JPEG when a PNG would be larger than about 2 MB, and scaled down only if still needed. Both are subject to browser storage limits.

When you [send screenshot feedback to your agent](/docs/send-to-your-agent/), download the ZIP and attach the matching image files separately. Review each image before sharing because it includes everything visible in the selected region or page, including form values and embedded frames.
